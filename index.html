Performance & Process KPIs

Trades History
—
Goal: 100+
Count of closed sell orders. Partial fills of the same IBKR order are one trade. Estimated rows are listed but excluded from win rate until marked ibkr_fifo.
Win Rate
—
—
Wins ÷ (wins + losses). Flat trades are excluded. Each closed order counts once — a $20 scalp weighs the same as a $700 winner. Not dollar-weighted.
Profit Factor
—
Gross Wins ÷ Losses
Gross winning dollars ÷ gross losing dollars. Above 1 means winners outweigh losers in dollars, even if win rate is under 50%. Under 1 means the opposite.
Total Realized
—
YTD closed P&L
Sum of IBKR FIFO realized P&L on closed sells. Commissions are already inside IBKR realized_pnl. Open positions are not included.
Avg Win
—
—
Mean dollar P&L of winning orders, and mean percent versus the cost basis implied by exit price and IBKR realized P&L.
Avg Loss
—
—
Mean dollar P&L of losing orders (a negative number), and mean percent versus implied cost. Compared with Avg Win, this is the payoff ratio.
Expectancy / trade
—
Avg edge per trade
Total realized ÷ number of closed orders, including flats. Average dollars the book made or lost per closed order. Not a forecast.
Largest Win / Loss

Best

Worst
Best and worst single closed order in the sample. One outlier can dominate expectancy — read it next to the averages.
